Microfriends

Téo Perrier, France, 2025

Under the microscope I found a zoo of impossible creatures. I modelled them in 3D to give them a body again: small organic monsters, alive and a little absurd.

Téo Perrier is an emerging artist based in Saint-Étienne, France. Student at the École supérieure d'art et design de Saint-Étienne , he's part of the new movement of young digital artists.
