hzn (read “horizon") is an open, virtual gallery you explore in 3D. Each door on the ring opens onto an artist's room, the way you'd browse entries in an encyclopedia. It's a young, open initiative and a standing invitation: if you make art and would like a place to show it, there's room for you here.

Current artworks

  • Marginalia · Juan Bautista Aballay

    Years of doodles drawn in the margins of notebooks — quick, absurd, everyday.

  • Microfriends · Téo Perrier

    3D creatures pulled from under the microscope: wild, organic little monsters.

  • Light and Wood · Ali Garaki Nezhad

    Digital paintings on how light meets matter, by a pianist and colour-science researcher.
